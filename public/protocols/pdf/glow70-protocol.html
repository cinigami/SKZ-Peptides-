🔬 Reconstitution Protocol

Materials Needed:

  • 1x GLOW 70 (70mg blend) vial
  • Bacteriostatic Water (BAC Water)
  • Insulin syringes (29G-31G)
  • Alcohol swabs
  • Sterile work environment

Step-by-Step Reconstitution:

  1. Preparation
    • Wash hands thoroughly
    • Clean work surface with alcohol
    • Allow vials to reach room temperature (10-15 minutes)
    • Remove protective caps from both vials
  2. Sterilize
    • Wipe rubber stoppers of both vials with fresh alcohol swabs
    • Allow to air dry for 30-60 seconds
    • Do NOT blow on or wipe dry
  3. Reconstitution
    • Draw 2mL BAC Water into a sterile syringe
    • Insert needle into GLOW 70 vial at an angle
    • Inject BAC Water slowly down the inside wall of the vial
    • Do NOT spray directly onto powder
    • Gently swirl or roll between palms — do NOT shake
    • Allow to dissolve completely (3-5 minutes) until solution is clear
    • Note: Solution may have a slight blue-green tint from the copper peptide — this is normal
  4. Label & Store
    • Label vial with: GLOW 70, concentration, date reconstituted
    • Store immediately at 2-8°C (refrigerator)
    • Protect from light

⚠️ Important Safety Considerations

Potential Research Observations:

  • Headache
  • Skin breakouts / acne (from GHK-Cu stimulating skin turnover — common in acne-prone individuals)

⚠️ "Copper Uglies" Warning

GHK-Cu (copper peptide) can cause a temporary skin purging phase sometimes called "copper uglies" — where skin may look worse before it gets better. This typically occurs in weeks 1-3 and includes:

  • Increased breakouts or acne flare-ups
  • Temporary skin redness or irritation
  • Slight swelling or puffiness

This is a normal part of the skin remodeling process. The skin is accelerating cell turnover and collagen production. Most users see significant improvement after week 3-4. If symptoms are severe, reduce dose by 50% until they resolve.

🚫 Who Should Avoid:

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Active cancer diagnosis — BPC-157 promotes angiogenesis which may feed tumors
  • Under 18 years of age
  • Wilson's disease or copper metabolism disorders (due to GHK-Cu)
  • Known allergy to copper or any peptide component

⚠️ Drug Interactions — Use Caution With:

  • Strong acids (AHAs/BHAs): Glycolic acid, salicylic acid — may degrade copper peptide if applied topically near injection site
  • High-strength Vitamin C serums: Can interfere with copper peptide activity
  • Retinoids (tretinoin, retinol): Combined with copper peptide may cause excessive skin irritation
  • Note: These interactions are primarily relevant for topical use near injection sites. Systemic interactions are minimal.

✅ Safety Tips:

  • Start low — Begin with 0.3mL to assess tolerance to all 3 peptides simultaneously
  • Rotate injection sites — Prevents localized tissue irritation
  • Don't exceed recommended copper peptide dose — More GHK-Cu is not always better; excessive copper can be pro-oxidant
  • Be patient with skin changes — "Copper uglies" are temporary; real improvements show weeks 4-8
  • Separate topical actives — If using AHAs/retinoids topically, apply them at a different time of day from injection
