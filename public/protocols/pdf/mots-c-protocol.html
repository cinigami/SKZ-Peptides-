🔬 How MOTS-c Works

MOTS-c is a 16-amino-acid peptide encoded in the mitochondrial genome. It acts as a metabolic regulator through the following mechanisms:

  • AMPK Pathway Activation: Activates AMP-activated protein kinase, the body's master metabolic switch
  • Glucose Regulation: Enhances cellular glucose uptake and insulin sensitivity
  • Fat Metabolism: Promotes fat oxidation and shifts energy utilization
  • Mitochondrial Function: Improves mitochondrial efficiency and ATP production
  • Exercise Mimetic: Replicates some molecular benefits of exercise at the cellular level
  • Stress Response: Activates adaptive cellular stress response pathways

🔬 Reconstitution Protocol

Materials Needed:

  • 1x MOTS-c 40mg vial
  • Bacteriostatic Water (BAC Water)
  • Insulin syringes (29G-31G)
  • Alcohol swabs
  • Sterile work environment

Step-by-Step Reconstitution:

  1. Preparation
    • Wash hands thoroughly
    • Clean work surface with alcohol
    • Allow vials to reach room temperature (10-15 minutes)
    • Remove protective caps from both vials
  2. Sterilize
    • Wipe rubber stoppers of both vials with fresh alcohol swabs
    • Allow to air dry for 30-60 seconds
    • Do NOT blow on or wipe dry
  3. Reconstitution
    • Draw 2mL BAC Water into a sterile syringe
    • Insert needle into MOTS-c vial at an angle
    • Inject BAC Water slowly down the inside wall of the vial
    • Do NOT spray directly onto powder
    • Gently swirl or roll between palms — do NOT shake
    • Allow to dissolve completely (2-5 minutes) until solution is clear
  4. Label & Store
    • Label vial with: MOTS-c, concentration, date reconstituted
    • Store immediately at 2-8°C (refrigerator)
    • Protect from light

🔬 Research Background

MOTS-c (Mitochondrial Open Reading Frame of the 12S rRNA-c) is a 16-amino-acid peptide encoded within the mitochondrial genome. Discovered in 2015 by Dr. Changhan David Lee at USC, it represents a new class of signaling molecules — mitochondrial-derived peptides (MDPs).

Key research findings:

  • Activates AMPK, the body's master metabolic regulator
  • Enhances glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity in animal models
  • Improves exercise capacity and endurance
  • Supports healthy aging by combating age-related metabolic decline
  • Acts as an "exercise mimetic" — replicating some molecular benefits of physical activity
  • Analog CB4211 shown to be "safe and well tolerated" in human trials
